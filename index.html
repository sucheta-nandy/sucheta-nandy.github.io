About

I'm pursuing an MSE in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Pennsylvania, building on more than three years of industry experience as a Data Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services. My interests sit at the intersection of machine learning, AI systems, data engineering, natural language processing, and intelligent information retrieval.

Most recently, I worked as a Data Engineering Master's Intern at Honeywell, where I built an enterprise natural language-to-SQL system using Python, Databricks, LangGraph, Neo4j, and Graph RAG. I developed retrieval, evaluation, validation, and monitoring workflows that helped the system achieve 79% accuracy while reducing manual query-development effort by 40%. The work also contributed to an internal IP/patent disclosure.

At Penn, I work as a Graduate Teaching Assistant and study machine learning, NLP, AI, statistics, and large-scale data systems. I enjoy problems that require both modeling and engineering: understanding user intent, retrieving the right information, evaluating model behavior, and building reliable systems around real-world data.

My portfolio applies the same end-to-end approach across data engineering, AI products, machine learning, NLP, and applied analytics. Recent work includes a PySpark ETL pipeline, an adaptive travel-review product, biomedical text curation, predictive modeling, and sports decision tools. I emphasize reproducible pipelines, honest evaluation, clear limitations, and usable outputs.

Outside of tech, I love sneakers, cars, traveling, discovering new food, and finding interesting places to explore.
